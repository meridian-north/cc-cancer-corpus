## Why your voice matters here

The drug-herb interaction table in this repository is technically accurate
and evidence-based. But a table of citations is not the same as a clinical
voice that says: "I have sat with patients in this situation. Here is what
I have seen. Here is what I have learned."

That voice is what turns a research tool into something a frightened patient
at 2 a.m. will actually trust. The table earns credibility. Your statement
earns trust. Both are needed.
