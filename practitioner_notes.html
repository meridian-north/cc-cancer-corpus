FOR YOUR CARE CONVERSATION

Private notes for my licensed practitioner

Put your observations and questions in order. Keep the note yourself, then share it directly with the licensed practitioner you choose through their secure channel.

There is no submit button. Entries are not sent to GitHub or a model. The worksheet does not save entries in application storage. Downloaded notes are ordinary, unencrypted text. Do not post personal health information in this repository.

Every field is optional. Record what already happened; this is not a treatment plan or a request to try something. For urgent concerns, use your practitioner’s usual urgent-care route.

Record actual dose, units, route and formulation if known. “Unknown” is useful. For example: relative to treatment, meals, sleep or symptoms. Include no change or unwanted effects. Separate observations from possible explanations. Other medicines, diet, activity, sleep, illness or treatment changes. A review does not mean that every risk has been ruled out. DOI, PMID, trial identifier or title. An AI answer is a lead to check, not a source.
