# Private notes for your licensed practitioner

Use the [local worksheet](practitioner_notes.html) to organize observations and questions for a licensed practitioner you choose: your treating clinician, oncology pharmacist, or a coordinated second-opinion practitioner. You can download a plain-text copy and share it through their established secure channel. The worksheet has no submission function, analytics, account or connection to GitHub.

**Do not submit personal information to this repository** through issues, pull requests, discussions, attachments or project email. Removing a name does not necessarily make a medical story anonymous. Public GitHub history and copies can persist after deletion.

The worksheet keeps entries in the current page without application storage. Browser recovery, extensions, shared devices and downloaded files have their own privacy risks. A downloaded note is ordinary, unencrypted text; keep it in a location you control. Loading the hosted page contacts the website host; the worksheet code does not transmit the entries. For offline use, download the repository ZIP and open `practitioner_notes.html` locally.

## What to record privately

- Your question and what you would like your practitioner to help decide.
- Treatment and other medicines, supplements or products already used; actual dose, units, route and formulation if known. Record uncertainty rather than guessing.
- Relative timing, such as before or after an infusion or symptom; exact dates are optional for your private clinical record.
- What you observed, when, and how it was measured. Separate a lab result or symptom from your interpretation of its cause. Include no change, unwanted effects and missing measurements.
- Other changes that could matter: other medicines, sleep, diet, activity, infection or treatment changes.
- Which licensed practitioner has reviewed the question, if anyone; unresolved concerns and agreed follow-up. A review is not a blanket safety clearance.
- Published source identifiers and questions about how closely those studies match your situation.

This is an observation aid, not a treatment plan or an invitation to start an experiment. Do not change treatment, dose or activity to fill in the worksheet. It is not monitored for urgent problems.

## How this can help research

A practitioner can help translate an observation into a general literature question. A **general question without personal history** or a published DOI/PMID/NCT can be proposed through [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md). For example: “Which controlled studies examine melatonin with platinum-based chemotherapy, and which outcomes were measured?”

Personal records remain with the person and their chosen practitioners. This repository does not collect case reports, run a trial, aggregate patient counts, pay for patient data or train models on those notes. Any future research collection would require a separate protocol, consent, governance and appropriate institutional review. A personal improvement or an absence of observed harm cannot establish causality or safety.
