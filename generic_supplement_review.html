Purpose: This reference indexes what is known, unknown, and lore about supplements and therapies in a cancer treatment context. It does not replace your oncologist, pharmacist, or your own judgment.

Evidence levels: RCT > clinical trial > observational > preclinical > lore > none/contested. Evidence grade is stated on every card. Absence of evidence is documented — not dismissed.

Interactions: Drug interaction signals reflect CYP enzyme, P-glycoprotein, and transporter pharmacology. WARN = active conflict documented. CAUTION = plausible mechanism, limited clinical data. WATCH = monitor. LOW = no known signal.

Lore: Traditional and anecdotal uses are included where they inform what patients actually do. Regional cultural weight varies. We remain the tool — you remain the decision-maker.

Regulation: Supplement regulation differs by country. Nothing here constitutes legal, regulatory, or clinical approval of any substance.

Data source: Corpus of 6,358 indexed studies. Query tab connects to the live index. SHA-256 verification available.
